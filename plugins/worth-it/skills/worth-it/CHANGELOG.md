# What's new in worth-it

Newest first. The first line under each date is what the skill shows users after an update, so keep it to one plain sentence.

## 2026-11-24
When the same kind of post keeps coming, worth-it may ask you one short question, so its verdicts fit you better. Answers are also shorter now.

## 2026-11-19 (7)
On a Mac without Homebrew, worth-it now explains the one-time install instead of failing quietly.

## 2026-11-19 (6)
Want worth-it on your phone? Say "set up my phone" and it does the setup for you from your computer.

## 2026-11-19 (5)
Phone setup is now one sentence, and phone sessions keep worth-it up to date by themselves.

## 2026-11-19 (4)
New users are told up front that the first link takes a few minutes, and the setup starts while they answer the onboarding questions.

## 2026-11-19 (3)
Safer link handling: worth-it only downloads real web links, uses your browser sign-in only for the big social sites, and checks the speech model download.

## 2026-11-19 (2)
worth-it now reminds you of your open to-do items once a week, so good ideas don't get forgotten. Say "weekly review" any time.

## 2026-11-19
worth-it is now a Claude Code plugin: it updates itself, and tells you here what changed.
- Your profile, lessons, log and to-do now live in the plugin's data folder, so updates never touch them.
- Onboarding offers to turn on automatic updates.
